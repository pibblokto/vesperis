## Request

The user's request of 2026-10-21 (quoted whole in R-421): "One interesting request/idea for planets - consider rogue planets
that lost their system and just fly in space. They shoulbe be able to be any kind of available planets and the only way to
find them is by radio signal (they should be very unqiues, comsic horror like sounds). If rogue planet is civilized desert (it
shouldn't be felisian since it would be cold already but you can add dead felisian world also among these 30-50 new types)
then civilization shards should tell how they planet lost the system, sun disappeared and they died (should be unique for
rogue planets)."

What exists: no body without a star. Every signal is a star's (the lock, the jump, the arrival and the save find the source
through the star of its sector); a jump ends at a star by rule (R-407); the ground's light has a floor (0.62) that keeps a
dim star's day readable. The radar's natural voices are synthesised from a seed (the magnetosphere already whistles, chirps,
hisses and roars).
