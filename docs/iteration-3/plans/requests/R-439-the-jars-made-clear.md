# R-439: the jars made clear

status: done 2026-11-19 (1.5.0, before its release; one of four requests for the incubator and the collection, R-439 to R-442)
milestone: 1.5.0

## Request

The user, 2026-11-19: "In incubator what jars tab is for? I don't understand it". Answered (a jar is a Winogradsky column: a world's wet
silt sorting itself into bands by what each kind lives on) and talked through: the page said none of it, its bands had no names, the
deep's grey band hardly showed on the silt, nothing a jar showed reached the sheets, and an eater of methane that needs air lay in the
airless deep. "Yes please proceed with all these points".

## Done

- **The bands' places** (`life/jars`): by what each lives on (`JarLayer`): the light's oxygen-makers, the air's edge (the iron's and every
  other eater of a chemical that needs air), the light without air, the sulphide's, the deep, the foot; each place's bands under the one
  before, never over another (they overlapped where two places' fixed tops met). The deep's grey a stone's, lighter than the silt.
- **The page:** a band come in is numbered beside the glass with a bar in its colour (a dark one's lit enough to see) and named under the
  list: its species' name, its look where seen (else AN UNSEEN KIND) and what it lives on ("1 A PLATE COLONY: LIGHT, MAKES OXYGEN"). An
  empty place says what a jar does. A jar's settling is said once ("THE JAR OF WET SILT HAS SETTLED - 2 BANDS").
- **The sheet:** a band seen on the page goes to its species' sheet (the guide's `jarband` line): "IN A JAR: AT THE TOP, LIVES ON
  LIGHT, MAKES OXYGEN".
