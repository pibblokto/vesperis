## Questions to talk through

1. **The cold worlds** (the largest share). Choices: (a) let a brine line live colder: a line set to BRINE with its FROST gene cut (an
   antifreeze) grows down to about -45 C, as perchlorate brines stay liquid on Mars; (b) let the drone seed underground, in rock or
   under ice, where a world's inner warmth keeps water liquid (the icy moons' hidden seas); (c) both.
2. **Venus and the acid worlds.** A venusian world's ground is about 460 C; its own life lives in its clouds. Choices: let the drone let
   a culture go into the cloud deck (a new place, THE CLOUDS, about 30 to 70 C, acid droplets), for acid life the player brings from such
   worlds; or also a gene that lets water life live in acid.
3. **Oxygen and pH**, fixed today: leave them (choose another culture), or let a gene move them.
4. **The furnaces** stay closed (nothing lives on molten rock), unless a world has a cool zone or a cloud deck.
5. **Greening a desert.** Today a light-feeder with DRY, its STOP cut and BIND covers sand in a carpet of its pigment's colour (green
   with a green pigment) but grows no plants. Choices: a carpet that grows grass and shrubs where it lies; or a made kind of its own on
   the data sheet (GREENED DESERT); or both.

## Decided

The user, 2026-11-22, on the five questions with a recommendation each: "Proceed". Taken:

1. **The cold worlds:** both. A brine line with no frost of its own grows down to -45 C; the drone seeds down a drill hole, in the
   rock's warmth or a sea under the ice (J-13).
2. **Venus:** THE CLOUDS a place to seed, for acid life brought from such worlds; no gene lets water life live in acid (J-14).
3. **Oxygen and pH:** fixed; a culture that suits them is chosen.
4. **The furnaces:** closed.
5. **Greening a desert:** both: a carpet that grows grass and shrubs where it lies, and GREENED DESERT (J-15).
