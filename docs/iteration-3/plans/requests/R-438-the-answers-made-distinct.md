# R-438: the answers made distinct

status: done 2026-11-19 (1.5.0, before its release; one of four requests for the laboratory's experiments, R-435 to R-438)
milestone: 1.5.0

## Request

The user, 2026-11-19: "More distinct effects of light/magnets/food/heat. It refers to previous point actually - 99% of time when you do
those experiments nothing happens, little guys don't react meaningfully to neither of options. Fels underwhelming, should be addressed".
Measured before it was built (their own eight vials; 254 drops of 80 living worlds): the light moved five cells or more on 9% of slides,
the food on 16%, the magnet on 6%; their sea world's slides were still at the vial's warmth, every species there past its range. Talked
through: every cell that turns answers, the dark's swimmers flee a bright light, the stage cools as well as warms and a kind moves only in
its range at its warmth's pace, lit light-feeders draw the eaters (Engelmann's, last). "Proceed and for your questions lets go with your
recommendations".

## Done

- **Every turner answers:** every cell within its reach that turns to the light or the food runs to it (no longer a crowd one cell deep:
  one cell at the first look's zoom), packed round the spot to twice its radius; the food's scent spreads from the drop (each cell drawn as
  its front reaches it).
- **The dark's swimmers flee a bright light:** a swimmer of soil, silt, a vent, rock or a plume that feeds on no light swims out of the
  spot.
- **The warmth:** a kind moves while the stage's warmth is in its range (a still one wakes, a moving one stops in the cold or the heat), its
  pace by its warmth (its species' speed at the vial's, up to twice at its best); past its top it is stressed, still or not.
- **Lit light-feeders draw the eaters** (Engelmann 1882): a spot on living light-feeders draws the eaters round them from ten seconds on.
- A magnetotactic cell answers the field alone; the magnet stays rare to answer, as in a real drop, and says where its cells live.
- Measured on the same drops: the light moves five cells or more on 32%, the food on 31% at 30 s; no world or species changes
  (generation 17 kept).
- Proof and records: `docs/decisions/log/7-1.5.0.md`, R-438; the unit's `experiments` group; `lab microscope`'s strips (cool, flee, lit).
