## Request

The user's request of 2026-10-21 (quoted whole in R-421): "each planet should have way more unique modifiers (again they
should make sense) - 20 to 30 more unique modifiers in addition to what we have now, also greater variery of modifiers in
general."

What exists: thirty-three traits (R-304, R-305, S-03, C-01): eighteen landforms, thirteen world traits, glassed and dead
civilisation; up to three a world (none for a fifth, one for two fifths, two for a bit over a quarter, three for the rest).

## Plan

`docs/ideas/IDEAS-worlds-and-wanderers.md` section 2; the milestones in the iteration file:

* **Q-01:** six slots a world (the three of today kept as they are, three new by kind), the nine facts read from the orbit
  and the spin (sideways spin, retrograde spin, racing day, locked face, eccentric year, strong field, no field, captured,
  twin, ringed) on a line of their own, and eleven landforms (great shield, tesserae, labyrinth, hollows, crater chains,
  penitentes, columnar basalt, mud volcanoes, inverted rivers, pingos, maars).
* **Q-02:** water (great tides, hot springs, pink lakes, milky seas), sky (noctilucent clouds, ice halos, airglow, fog seas,
  great aurorae), life (barrier reefs, fungal forests, petrified forests, algal blooms), ground (rainbow strata, gypsum
  sands, meteorite fields).
* **Q-03:** sound (singing dunes, singing ice, quakes).
* **D-04:** the four only a drifter has (frozen air, embers, lights left on, lights in the water).

Thirty new traits, six kinds of trait where there were two, plus the facts and the drifters' four.
