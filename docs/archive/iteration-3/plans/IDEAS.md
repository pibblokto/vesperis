# Ideas backlog, third iteration

Unscheduled; promote by asking.

## Belts and small bodies
* Landing on a belt rock or a captured moonlet: the small-body surface code (O4) would carry it; the capsule needs a "tether" landing and the site a rock-sized map.
* Mining or sampling: the analyzer names a rock's composition (stony, iron, carbonaceous) from its hash; a sample counter in the guide.
* Rocks that occlude the sun as they pass (eclipses in the belt) and collide with the ship's hull with a thump.

## Comets
* A comet's tail seen from the ground changing over the plunge (longer near periapsis), meteors on nearby worlds when they cross its orbit.
* Landing on the night side only when the activity is high: the day side vents too hard.

## Sectors and maps
* Naming sectors in the guide; a "sectors visited" statistic and a map that shades them.
* A route planner: waypoints chained on the sector map with the total walking time.

## Terrain (see `PLAN-terrain-3.md`)
* Caves and overhangs (displaced meshes or a second height field): out of scope for O6 by the user's decision of 2026-09-27; the next iteration's. O6-05/O6-06 leave pit chains and cave mouths as surface features that can be opened later.
* Rivers you can follow to the sea; canyon rims as landmarks visible from orbit.
