# Rules: the overgrowth

Rules for what a seeded line's overgrowth makes of a world (1.5.0, R-448: J-09 on), one line per rule with its log entries (`log/`); a
replaced rule moves to `superseded.md`. The made air, waters, ground and plants and the animals' fates are in `rules-life-made.md`.

## The cover and its structures
- A line that never stops and binds covers the land it lives on (films if it slimes alone) from its step 8, the world by its 32nd [J-09]
- Its type by its genes and way of living, its looks by its genes; each growing line its own colour and bank where it lies [J-09], [B-457]
- Its edge is fixed to the place and only moves out; never on water, snow, ice or lava; a forest keeps its trees, wrapped [J-09]
- Its structures stand on a world-fixed lattice (a cube map's 16 m cells); their feet the colliders' and the drawing's; no tree in one [J-09], [B-461]
- A cell's structures stand where the cover holds its four corners, read on the world; a cell across a cube's edge is the face beyond's [B-483], [B-484]
- A structure grows from the line whose cover lies at its cell's middle: its type, sizes and colour [B-457]
- A vial off a structure says what it was taken from (TISSUE FROM A MOUND); its microhabitat is the cover's [B-461]
- A light-feeder's carpet or lichen on a bare world with air (no plants or seas of its own) grows grass and shrubs, from 4 steps behind its edge [J-15], [B-482]
- Thick from 12, in its pigment's colour (a dye's over it), its carpet's too, in its line's own bank; no tree in them, a land look under [J-15], [B-482]
- The editor's cut STOP says what a line overgrows as (`overgrowthTypeOf`, the layer's own rule) and that a carpet or a lichen greens a desert [J-15]

## The ruins
- Its cover climbs each building by the steps since its edge passed, faster as it goes, a wall more on the side it came from [J-10], [B-462]
- It counts its steps on past 24 to 64, so a ruin reached late is still taken; a settlement on snow, ice, water or lava stays bare [J-10]
- Drawn in the forms' second bank under the climb; a face taken whole in the cover's look and light, its windows shut [J-10], [B-462], [B-466]
- At night a face under a glowing cover is lit dimly and brightly in its veins, as the ground's; a face its edge crosses has the cover's light [B-486]
- Membranes across the doorways, windows, gates and arches' bays it climbed half way up; nodules; drapes from the tops [J-10], [B-463]
- Its pieces never block: the colliders stay the ruins' own; a membrane pushed through tears once and stays torn the landing [J-10], [B-464]
- Records, instruments and lit pieces stay bare; a world nobody seeded draws its ruins as before [J-10]
- A stone rotter eats the earth, stone, brick and concrete of each building it reached where its line lives, over twelve steps [J-10], [B-465]
- On the ground the ruins read the world now (the game hands it on): a step passing grows them on [B-465]
- The ground takes the world now too where what its samples read moved: the rings sampled again, its cover, looks and structures with it [B-485]
- Each building takes the line whose cover lies at its own place: its colour and bank, its tile, its pieces' grain, its light at night [B-457], [B-488]

## The sound
- A wet overgrowth (0.3 and over) squelches underfoot (WET GROUND); a flesh's or a sponge's vents breathe at their lips' pace (BREATHING) [J-10]
- The tall and the hard near creak in the wind and hair hisses, as much as the air carries; their levels sit beside the wind's [J-10]
- Where every animal is gone (SILENCE) nothing living is heard: no birds, calls, hoofs or insects [J-10]
