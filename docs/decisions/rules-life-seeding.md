# Rules: sending a culture down and a seeded world's record

Rules for sending a culture down to a world and for what the explorer keeps of a seeded world (1.5.0, R-448: J-03, J-12), one line per
rule with its log entries (`log/`); a replaced rule moves to `superseded.md`. The world genes and the seeded worlds' layer are in
`rules-life-world.md`; what the lines make of a world in `rules-life-made.md`, `rules-life-overgrowth.md` and `rules-life-kinds.md`.

## Sending a culture down
- S on the drone bay sends drone 1 alone with a flask that shows FULL to a place on the map; the flask keeps its culture [J-03]
- Refused at home (the pinned star's first felisian planet), a giant, out of orbit, the drones away or charging, three lines living [J-03]
- The culture is let go as the drone arrives (its `seeded` line then); called back before that, nothing went down [J-03]
- The verdict is a pure function of world, line and place, its medium and growth the incubator's (`mediumOfPlace`, `growthIn`) [J-03]
- Its order: furnace, liquid, medium, oxygen, pH, dose (SHIELD raises it), land (DRY), natives; the first failure is said [J-03]
- A native a tenth quicker there outgrows the line; a slower one it outgrows (fewer in samples); between, they live side by side [J-03]
- A line that takes drifts by a hash of world, line and place: one or two genes, never a vital one, twice as often under strong UV [J-03]
- A spread keeps to its line's waters (by salt), its land (DRY, or snow and ice) and its band of warmth, at its doubling's pace [J-03]
- A dye colours the waters a line lives in (the latest dye's), a glowing line lights them at night, from step 1; a layer of no look has no key [J-03]
- A vial keeps the guide's clock at its take: it holds a line only where the line had spread by then [J-03]

## The record
- A native a line beat at its place (outgrown, eaten or poisoned) dies out once the line covers the world, its 24th step (`seedingDiedOut`) [J-12]
- Only one living in no niche or salt the line does not: a water's salts among the line's waters, any other niche where it lives on land [J-12]
- No vial of the world holds a native that died out, wherever taken; one beaten that lives on elsewhere is a hundredth where the line spread [J-12]
- A line seen under the microscope in a vial of the world it went down on is found wild: the guide's `wild` line (`<world>/L<k>`), SEEDED logged [J-12]
- The sequencer reading it from there marks its record read (the save's `seqwildkey`); the KIN line says what the wild changed, or UNCHANGED [J-12]
- By world, a seeded world's head names its made kind, a native seen that died out reads DIED OUT, the lines found wild follow its species [J-12]
- A wild line's sheet is its species' on its form as the wild left it: when it went down, what the wild changed once read there [J-12]
- Every world's animals seen follow, with the marks the ground showed (GONE over DYING, then CHANGED); an animal's sheet is in words alone [J-12]
- A species gone from its world is drawn dim; by kin lists the microbes seen alone, as before [J-12]
- The LIFE page adds WORLDS SEEDED, MADE and DIED OUT as it opens: the catalogue's DIED OUT rows, a friend's lent never counted [J-12]
- Auto-explore never seeds, and lands on a seeded world as on any other: it reads nothing of the layer but its map [J-12]
