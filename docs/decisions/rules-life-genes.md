# Rules: the genes and the gene editor

A species' genes changed aboard (1.5.0, R-443: Y-02 to Y-04), a line each with its entries (`log/`); a replaced one moves to
`superseded.md`. Where life is: `rules-life.md`; the incubator and the collection: `rules-life-cultures.md`; the sequencer, the tree
and the record: `rules-life-record.md`.

## The genes
- A species' genes are read from what it is (its values, its form's width and length) and its genesis: nothing of generation 17 moves [Y-02]
- A genesis's map (18 places: 3 vital, 3 quiet, warmth, salt, size, pigment, food, motion, 3 senses, rest, glow, shape) is every kin's [Y-02]
- Its code: each letter's weight (home's A 0, T 1, C 2, G 3) and its tables, each value in a table once at least; salts of their own [Y-02]
- A species has the genes it uses, a gap where it lacks one; its wild letters give its own values by its genesis's code [Y-02]
- An unedited line is its species bit for bit: its values, its culture's segments, its slide's kinds (the unit, forty worlds) [Y-02]
- An edit moves a value from its parent's by the change in the code: a warmth's step a weight, a size two to the third a weight [Y-02]
- A gene cut loses its work (its warmth round its best, fresh, half its size; no pigment, food, motion, sense, rest, glow; a sphere) [Y-02]
- A vital gene changed or cut: the line does not live (its cells lyse in seconds, `GF_UNVIABLE`); a quiet gene changes nothing [Y-02]
- How it lives and its plan's family never move; a shape among a small cell's four, a motion among its plan's; values in their limits [Y-02]
- A strain's genes are its parent's with its move's gene stepped its way: the sequencer reads which gene its move was in [Y-02]

## The editor and its lines
- A machine of its own (`EDITOR`, E at the bow bench): a culture read on the sequencer, a flask's or a kept one; another refused [Y-02]
- An edited line is a strain of its species (its `strain` line SM_EDIT and its edits; its number its species' next, drawn E) [Y-02]
- A line is read everywhere a strain is: its flask's culture and form, its drop, the sequencer, the collection, its world's tree [Y-02]
- W writes in EDIT_S (120 s) on the lab's clock, the screen open or not, into the first empty flask (none: the collection) [Y-02]
- Its letters by the arrows alone (P and T are the game's own keys: a photograph, the warp); its edits stay on it once written [Y-02]
- A parent's cut gene stays cut in its lines (edit the parent); a gap takes no gene till Y-03 moves one in [Y-02]
- An edited line's answers on the slide are its line's, never its species' sheet's [Y-02]

## Learning what a gene does
- A gene is NOT KNOWN till seen; what was seen is the guide's (`gene` lines, by genesis and number: every kin's) [Y-02]
- An edited line against its parent: its flask's fit, its end and its glow alone in a flask; its drop's shape, size, colour, motion [Y-02]
- Its answers to the light, the food and the field where its parent's would differ; a vital gene's death at once [Y-02]
- Of its changed genes those that could show it (known to do nothing else; NOTHING SEEN not for a drop's): one learned, more said [Y-02]
- Grown and looked at with nothing different: its changed genes not known NOTHING SEEN; a strain read: its move's gene learned [Y-02]
- A finding is logged at once (GENE: what was seen, what it says) and said in its turn, two lines of 51 characters, one at a time [Y-02]
