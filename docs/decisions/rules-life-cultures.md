# Rules: the incubator and the collection

Rules for growing microbes aboard (1.5.0, R-425: Z-05; R-433, R-435, R-440 to R-442, R-447, B-441, B-442), one line per rule with its log
entries (`log/`); a replaced rule moves to `superseded.md`. The search and the microscope are in `rules-life-lab.md`, the sequencer,
the tree and the record in `rules-life-record.md`, and where life is in `rules-life.md`.

## The incubator
- The incubator is a screen over the room (`INCUBATOR`, E at its cabinet); flasks and the lab's clock (like the drones') have their own save lines [Z-05]
- A culture is closed-form in its events on the lab's clock (`runCulture`): the same whether skipped, stepped or laid out again from saved events [Z-05]
- A flask takes a species seen in a vial (never a fossil or a protocell) or a flask's culture, at first in the medium of its world (`mediumOfPlace`) [Z-05]
- A species' pace comes from its seed: at best a flask is full in 2-5 minutes and stays full while its medium suits it; spores wake in 5-15 s [Z-05] [R-449]
- Two in a flask: mirrored hands ignore each other, an eater and a smaller cell cycle, a light-feeder or fermenter feeds the other, the rest compete [Z-05]
- In a predator cycle a switched strain's state follows its numbers: the cycle is split where it crosses its quorum share [B-446]
- Twelve generations at a range's edge make a strain with one value moved, as an event at its own time; the guide keeps it (`strain`, named with N) [Z-05]
- What happens in a flask is said and logged as the clock reaches it (WOKEN, STRAIN; the glow is said); what the curve shows goes to the sheet [Z-05]
- A flask's warmth is typed on its page (a digit starts the entry) and set as one change of the medium, within the range its cell holds [R-435]
- Water life grows down to -20 C; a line in brine with no ice of its own (FROST none, cut or off) to -45 C, and a brine flask with it [J-13]
- Read once every edit is in, in any order; a band edited lower is lifted whole; a warmth gene moved in keeps its donor's cold [J-13]
- N on a flask names its organism; with two (or a strain) the explorer first chooses which. The name is the guide's, as on the microscope [R-442]
- In the lab's rows an unnamed species is shown by its look (with its size in a chooser); rows still alike add colour and motion, then a number [B-442]
- The incubator and sequencer choosers have two tabs, the vials by world and the cultures, and open on the tab last shown [R-441]
- No jars: an older save's `jar` lines and an older guide's `jarband` lines are dropped when loaded [R-447]
- Flask tones use home keys past the banks (`ShipBanks`, 32-47), asked for only in front of the eye and after the screens near them [Z-05]
- A drop from a flask on the microscope is the culture as it is now; vial and flask slides read one list of species, keys and worlds [Z-05]
- On the lab's shelves a flask past the ship's banks takes the dealt bank nearest its colour (a fifth of the cube); none near, it dims [B-459]

## The collection
- Each species and strain a flask grows (curve past the lag) is kept once with its media, flask emptied or not, to be staged, put in a flask or read [R-433]
- The explorer makes the collection's folders (N makes, R renames, M moves, Backspace removes an empty one), saved as `keptfolder` and `kept` lines [R-433]
- A species that grew on its partner's gift is kept with the partner, and the two are staged and put in together; a drop says whether its cells live [B-441]
- A kept culture is one species or a pair; a pair is one entry either way round (a load merges two), and a folder holds a culture only once [R-440]
- K keeps a copy in a folder: the cell under the crosshair alone (a vial's in its world's medium), a flask's one species, one or both of two; C copies [R-440]
