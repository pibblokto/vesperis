# Ideas backlog, second iteration

Unscheduled. The first iteration's leftovers sit in roadmap N5; the rest here, cheap to wild.

## Worlds
* Weather fronts you can see coming: a rain wall on the horizon, a dust storm's brown edge.
* Caves and overhangs under cliffs (needs displaced meshes; big).
* Geysers on icy moons, tar pits, salt flats that crust.
* Volcanic eruptions visible from orbit as glow points on the night side.

## Life
* Predators that hunt the herds at dusk (a chase you can watch from the buggy).
* Migrations: herds moving with the season across the sector map.
* Bioluminescent forests at night on some worlds.
* A creature that follows you home to the capsule.

## Ship and interface
* A telescope view from the ship (zoom on the crosshair) to inspect distant bodies and comets.
* The Stardrifter as a moving point in the surface sky (it is in orbit).
* Postcards: a PNG with the frame plus a strip of coordinates and time.

## Presentation
* An in-game key rebinding screen (N5-05) and gamepad rebinding.
* Colour-blind HUD schemes.

## Engineering
* Integer noise for cross-platform determinism (KI-014).
* Data tables (colours, species, families) in a text file for tweaking without recompiling.
